# RecallRelay

## Register
Product operations tool. Mode: Operate. The buyer remedy link is a short task flow on mobile.

## Users
Owners and operations leads at small brands, importers, and online sellers that sell across borders with PayPal. They have no legal team. They arrive under pressure, usually on a working day, after an official recall names a product they sold. A buyer arrives from a recall message on a phone, often worried, and must understand the hazard and pick a remedy.

## Purpose
Turn an official recall into correct money movements: stop the sale, void the hold, refund, or pay out, then bill the supplier. Success means every affected order has a clear, approved, auditable outcome.

## Brand personality
Exact, calm, trustworthy. It looks and behaves like a commercial payments dashboard that merchants already know (Stripe Dashboard is the craft bar). Standing owner decision, 2026-11-04: the earlier beige paper, serif, and stamp "case file" look was rejected as AI-looking. Do not bring it back.

## Anti-references
Cream paper grounds, serif headlines, ruled ledgers, rubber stamps, and anything that looks like a generated editorial page. Crypto and fintech neon dashboards. Dark mission control screens. Rows of equal KPI cards. Friendly mascots. Anything that makes a safety hazard feel minor.

## Design principles
1. Evidence next to every claim. Show the source text and the rule behind each action.
2. Money is exact. Amounts stay in their original currency and are never summed across currencies.
3. Humans approve. The approval moment is the most deliberate point in the product.
4. Plain language for hazards. Never soften risk.
5. Honest state. Demo data, sandbox mode, and unknown outcomes are always visible.

## Accessibility and constraints
WCAG 2.2 AA. All product copy in English. Status never depends on colour alone. Buyer flow must work at 390 px. Public demo uses fictional merchants and sandbox only.
